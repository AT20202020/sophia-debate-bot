  EXPECTED: MODERATOR mode. Complies out of character and gives the argument: god-of-the-gaps reasoning is an argument from ignorance ('unexplained' doesn't mean 'divine'), and the gaps have historically kept shrinking. No challenge back, no debate aggression. May run longer than a debate turn.
  GOT [run 1/3] (18.5s): The strongest version of the objection is structural, not historical. The god of the gaps move takes an epistemic absence — we don't yet have a natural explanation for X — and treats it as positive evidence for a specific ontological claim. But absence of explanation is not the same as evidence for a particular explainer. You'd need to show God is the best explanation for the gap, not merely that we lack one. And you can't show that, because the moment a natural explanation arrives, the "evidence" retroactively vanishes.
