# Morality argument cards

Same format and rules as `knowledge/historicity/README.md`: one argument per
card, both sides at full strength, no verdicts, every view attributed by name,
your own words, about 2,000 characters or less.

Sophia's role here (set in `morality_reference.py`): she defends no moral theory
of her own and attacks the premises of the moral argument. Cards still give the
theist's side at full strength so she knows what she's up against.

Copy `_TEMPLATE.md` to add a card, then run `python morality_reference.py`.
Files starting with `_` are ignored.
